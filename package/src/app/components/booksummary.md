Christian Theologians, Philosophers and Scholars
Christian Theologians, Philosophers and Scholars: Their Lives, Works and Ideas, a research work by J.I Bamidele, introduces 104 individuals associated with Christian intellectual history, from the early Church to African and global Christianity.

The book explores their backgrounds, major writings, central ideas and influence. It traces early Christian foundations, medieval thought, the Reformation, modern theology, Christian philosophy, biblical scholarship and African contributions.

Designed for students, pastors, teachers and interested readers, it includes a historical overview, theological glossary, guided reading programme, reflection questions and alphabetical index. It encourages readers to understand thinkers in their historical settings, examine differing perspectives fairly and consult their original works.

It is a selective reference guide, rather than an exhaustive catalogue of every Christian thinker.
